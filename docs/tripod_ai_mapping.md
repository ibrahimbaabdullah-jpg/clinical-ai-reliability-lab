# TRIPOD+AI reporting map

**Evidence snapshot: 2026-10-12.** This is a working reporting map, not a claim of complete TRIPOD+AI adherence, registration, ethics approval or clinical validation. The [manuscript draft](manuscript_draft.md) reports the completed full public-cohort retrospective experiment; author review and declarations remain pending.

The completed [full-v1 report](../bundles/full-v1/report.md) contains **40,336 released patient records and 1,552,210 hourly rows**, all three fitted model families, 18 stress evaluations, four ablations and selective-withholding analyses. Hospital A contains 20,336 records; B contains 20,000. The six primary family-by-cohort evaluations each have 1,000 valid patient-bootstrap replicates for every reported interval. Full-run evidence is traceable through the [report JSON](../bundles/full-v1/report.json), [run manifest](../bundles/full-v1/run_manifest.json), [cohort profile](../reports/full-v1-cohort/cohort_profile.md) and [figure data](../reports/figures/full-v1/figure-data.json). Software verification is recorded separately in [verification notes](verification.md); passing checks does not establish clinical performance or complete reporting.

The earlier [pilot](../bundles/pilot-v1/report.md) used 2,400 records, 91,276 hours and 200 bootstrap replicates. Its 1,200 B records overlap the full external cohort. The full analysis follows the frozen development protocol, but is not wholly unseen confirmatory validation.

Reference: Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, et al. TRIPOD+AI statement: updated guidance for reporting clinical prediction models that use regression or machine learning methods. *BMJ*. 2024;385:e078378. [doi:10.1136/bmj-2023-078378](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-078378). The [official checklist](https://www.tripod-statement.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/TRIPODAI_checklist.pdf) is content-versioned 11 January 2024; the older year in its URL is not the checklist version.

The rows below are project-specific tracking descriptions, not a reproduction of the official checklist. Consult the checklist and its subitems before submission. Full-run evidence and preliminary pilot evidence retain separate run identities.

## Evidence package and remaining reporting work

- Completed analysis: [full report](../bundles/full-v1/report.json), [fitted bundle](../bundles/full-v1/), [cohort profile](../reports/full-v1-cohort/cohort_profile.md) and four PNG/SVG figure pairs with [source data](../reports/figures/full-v1/figure-data.json). The run's data hash is over canonical pandas row hashes; the profile's Parquet hash identifies its serialized input. These different hash bases are not interchangeable.
- Required author work: verify manuscript claims and references, finalize authorship/affiliations/contributions, provide funding/conflict/AI-assistance declarations, and obtain an institutional determination if applicable. No ethics approval, waiver or exemption is asserted.
- Reporting gaps: subgroup confidence intervals are absent; selective-policy and paired comparison uncertainty are also unestimated. Preserve these gaps rather than claim complete checklist adherence or equal performance.
- Release details: state the actual repository/model/Site access terms and version identifiers used for submission. The earlier pilot is separately retained and disclosed, including B overlap.

The completed study provides retrospective evidence on the released cohort. Smoke tests and synthetic replay provide software evidence. Prospective workflow performance, human responses to alerts/withholding and effects on patient outcomes remain unevaluated.
