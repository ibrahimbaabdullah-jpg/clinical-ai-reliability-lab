TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Methods & limitations

A research workbench for asking a precise question—and reporting where the evidence stops.

INTENDED USE

Retrospective model research and education.

This platform is for researchers and clinical informatics practitioners evaluating ICU sepsis prediction models. It is not a medical device, a clinical risk calculator, or a tool for patient-care decisions. The replay accepts only generated synthetic timelines.

01 / TASK

Reproduce the challenge first

PhysioNet Challenge 2019 labels become positive six hours before the reference sepsis onset and remain positive afterward. The original task and official utility metric are reproduced without shifting these labels again.

A separate early-warning analysis counts alerts in [onset − 6 hours, onset). Post-onset detections do not count as early warnings.

02 / SEPARATION

Keep development and evaluation apart

Hospital A is split by patient into training (55%), tuning (15%), calibration (10%), threshold selection (10%), and final test (10%) sets. Every patient in hospital B is held out for external evaluation.

Imputation and scaling use training data. Hyperparameters use tuning data. Calibration and the alert threshold use separate partitions.

03 / RELIABILITY

Stress the observations

Random missingness removes observations; delays postpone availability; measurement noise perturbs observed values using training-derived scales. Causal features are then rebuilt with no access to future observations.

Model families include regularized logistic regression, gradient-boosted trees, and a unidirectional GRU. The selected family is determined from development data.

04 / INTERPRETATION

Report denominators and failure modes

Report average precision, calibration, challenge utility, event sensitivity, lead time, and false-alert episodes per eligible non-warning patient-day. This denominator includes observed hours of never-septic patients and hours before onset − 6 in eligible septic patients, divided by 24. It excludes the warning window, post-onset time, and censored septic cases. Operational alerts use a six-hour cooldown.

Left-censored events and inferred onsets after follow-up are excluded from pre-onset analysis and counted explicitly. Small subgroup estimates are suppressed.

Evidence boundaries

  • Synthetic results establish that the software runs; they do not establish clinical utility, fairness, or safety.
  • Real retrospective performance does not establish prospective clinical benefit. The full-cohort release includes 1,200 hospital B records previously inspected in the pilot (6% of B); it is not a wholly new confirmatory validation.
  • Probability calibration, feature contributions, and selective prediction are empirical analyses, not guarantees.
  • Document data provenance, cohort restrictions, failed experiments, and limitations alongside all reported metrics.
